FAQ

How does Parenthink help track my child's growth?

Parenthink lets you log and monitor height, weight, and other growth milestones over time.

Can I customize activities for my child?

Yes. You can plan meals, playtime, naps, and learning activities around your child's routine.

Which devices does Parenthink support?

Parenthink 1.0 supports compatible iPhone and iPad devices. Android and browser data access are not currently available.

Can I set reminders for my child's daily tasks?

Yes. Set gentle reminders for meals, medication, vaccinations, homework, and more.
