Questions, answered

A few things parents usually ask.

Need help with your device, purchases, reminders, or family sharing?

Visit support
What can I track in Parenthink?

Track feeding and meals, pumping, sleep, growth, medication, vaccines, homework, activities, Family Notes, and your own custom routines.

Is Parenthink useful without Premium?

Yes. Free includes one child, unlimited core tracking, checklists, reminders, meal planning, custom recipes, private iCloud backup, and 40 parenting guides.

Which devices and languages are supported?

Parenthink supports compatible iPhone and iPad devices, plus a paired Apple Watch companion. The app is available in English, Indonesian, and Thai.

Does Parenthink diagnose health or development?

No. Parenthink organizes the information you enter and provides neutral summaries. It does not diagnose, prescribe, or replace professional medical advice.

How does family sharing work?

A Premium owner can create and manage an optional iCloud family workspace. Invited family members can contribute to an existing shared workspace without their own Premium subscription.
